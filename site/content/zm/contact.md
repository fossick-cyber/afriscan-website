### For a line, route or site in Zambia

- **The province and district**, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The asset:** a power line and its voltage, a petroleum pipeline, a railway or road, or a mining licence area, and the file you have for it.
- **The widths to report:** the SI 2 of 2026 wayleave for the voltage, the forestry width where a line crosses a recognised forestry area, your easement or reserve widths, or the consent distances around planned mine workings. Up to six widths in one survey.
- **The date the record must reflect**, such as a cut-off date, the date a wayleave was acquired or the start of rehabilitation.
- **Your own imagery:** whether your teams already fly drones, and the dates of the latest orthophotos.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs a Zambian operator with an ROC and an Air Services Permit, a special authorisation and Air Force clearance for each operation, so the proposal shows the lead time. See [drone law in Zambia](/zm/drone-regulations).
- **Protected status:** whether the corridor or site is a declared protected area or place, and who authorises work on it.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
