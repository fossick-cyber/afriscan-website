### For a route or site in Nigeria

None of this is needed to get in touch: it is what we'll ask about when we get back to you.

- **The states and LGAs** the line or site crosses, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The asset:** crude, gas or product pipeline (and the licence strip as a polygon, if you have it), or a power line and its voltage.
- **The widths to report:** the NESIS right of way for the voltage, the distance in any Oil Pipelines Act s.12 order, or your company or lender standard.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone surveys need NCAA and ONSA authorisations and, near high-tension lines, a special authorisation, so the proposal shows their lead time. See [drone law in Nigeria](/ng/drone-regulations).
- **The date the record must reflect**, such as a cut-off date, a handover date or the date a line was built.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
- **For upstream oil and gas work:** whether the contract needs NOGIC JQS registration, an NUPRC service permit or an NCDMB-approved Nigerian content plan, so the proposal can address each one.
- **For mini-grid site work:** the candidate communities or sites, as points or boundaries, and the household assumptions your programme uses, if any.
