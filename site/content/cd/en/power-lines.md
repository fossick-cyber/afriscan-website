    a: "The 1993 arrêté speaks of 25 metres \"de part et d'autre\", on each side, without saying what the distance is measured from: the centre line or the outer conductor.   - q: Do you measure the distance between vegetation and the conductors?

::::section{id="problem" eyebrow="The problem, in your words" title="Between two inspections, the emprise changes"}
:::cards{cols="2"}
:::card{title="“Structures go up under the line between patrols.”" icon="houses"}
A high-voltage line crosses kilometres of neighbourhoods, fields and bush. A structure found early can be handled through dialogue; found late, it becomes a compensation file or a dispute.
:::
:::card{title="“Vegetation is closing in on the emprise.”" icon="tree"}
Trimming branches near the conductors is among the operator's rights (Loi n° 14/011, art. 106). You still need to know where the tall vegetation is, segment by segment.
:::
:::card{title="“There is digging near the towers.”" icon="excavation"}
Fresh excavations, spoil, trenches and earthworks near the towers or along a cable route show at the surface and are flagged from one survey to the next.
:::
:::card{title="“We are planning a new route.”" icon="route"}
The approval file for a route includes a parcel plan of the land the servitudes will affect. Counting the structures along each option before the route is fixed is simpler than changing it afterwards.
:::

### The servitudes of the 1993 interministerial arrêté {#arrete-1993}

[Arrêté interministériel n° 0021 of 29 October 1993](https://www.leganet.be/Legislation/Droit%20civil/AM.021.29.10.1993.Servitude.htm), issued by the ministers for Land Affairs and for Public Works, Land-Use Planning, Town Planning and Housing, lists among the servitudes "les emprises des lignes de haute tension sur une distance de 25 mètres de part et d'autre", the emprises of high-voltage lines over 25 metres on each side, as well as "les périmètres REGIDESO, S.N.EL. et ONPTZ". It provides that "toute occupation, toute construction et tout lotissement", any occupation, construction or subdivision, is prohibited there (art. 2), and that structures built in breach "seront démolies aux frais de leurs constructeurs ou propriétaires sans aucune indemnité", will be demolished at their builders' or owners' expense without compensation (art. 3).

### Loi n° 14/011 on the electricity sector {#loi-14-011}

[Loi n° 14/011 of 17 June 2014](https://www.leganet.be/Legislation/Droit%20economique/Energie/Loi.14.011.17.06.2014.htm) sets out the servitudes of power lines:
- **Art. 106:** the right to "élaguer les branches d'arbres à proximité des conducteurs aériens", to trim tree branches near overhead conductors.
- **Art. 107:** the works are carried out "en accord avec les concessionnaires fonciers", in agreement with the holders of the land, and involve no dispossession.
- **Art. 108:** "Le passage des conducteurs au-dessus des concessions foncières ne fait pas obstacle au titulaire du droit foncier de le clôturer ou d'y bâtir": conductors passing over a land concession do not stop the holder of the land right from fencing it or building on it. A right of way for maintenance remains, without compensation.
- **Art. 109:** the holder gives three months' notice before demolishing, repairing, raising, fencing or building.
- **Art. 111:** the servitude is preceded by a notification and a record of condition (état des lieux) drawn up by the land administration.
- **Arts 112 and 113:** after a declaration of public utility, compensation follows the expropriation rules; a permanent emprise gives a right to compensation, including for "les occupants des terres rurales qui ont effectivement mis le fonds en valeur", occupants of rural land who have actually developed it.
- **Arts 114 to 116:** the route is approved after an inquiry, on a file that includes "un plan parcellaire indiquant toutes les concessions foncières ou terrains devant être frappés par les servitudes", a parcel plan showing every land concession or plot the servitudes will affect.
- **Art. 125:** penalises "quiconque construit sur ou sous les lignes électriques ou occupe des emprises des installations électriques du domaine public de l'État", anyone who builds on or under power lines or occupies the emprises of electrical installations in the State's public domain, and more heavily those "qui autorisent cette construction ou cette occupation", who authorise that construction or occupation.
- **Art. 132:** penalises obstructing the works or "l'usage par l'exploitant des servitudes", the operator's use of the servitudes.

::::section{id="new-lines" tone="alt" eyebrow="New lines" title="Before the parcel plan, count the structures along each option"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
For a new line or an interconnection, we count the structures along each route option, by band and by segment, so that the land impact weighs in the choice before the route is fixed. Once the route is chosen, a dated register of the structures inside the future servitudes prepares the parcel plan of the inquiry file (Loi n° 14/011, arts 114 to 116) and the record of condition that comes before the servitude (art. 111).

Where a lender applies IFC Performance Standard 5, the same register, made at a known date, supports the cut-off-date record. It replaces neither the census nor the asset inventory.

::::section{id="how" eyebrow="How it works" title="From the line's route to a checked register"}
:::steps
:::step{title="Scope"}
You send the route or the tower coordinates, the voltage and the widths to measure. We agree the bands, the imagery and the deliverables in a written proposal.
:::
:::step{title="Survey"}
Satellite imagery and open building datasets give a first register of the structures in each band.
:::
:::step{title="Check"}
An analyst confirms, corrects and adds to every result. Anything the imagery cannot settle is listed for checking on the ground.
:::
:::step{title="Deliver and repeat"}
The report and GIS files go to the contacts you name. Later surveys show what has changed since the previous one.

### What we do

:::checklist
- Record the structures, tracks and excavations visible from above in the bands you set
- Rate every 500 m segment for encroachment density
- Map tall vegetation inside the emprise
- Flag changes between two surveys, confirmed by an analyst
:::
::::
::::col
### What we don't do

:::checklist{tone="no"}
- Measure safety clearances to the conductors
- Decide whether a structure is permitted
- Identify, count or follow people
- Stand in for the inquiry, the record of condition or the authority's decision
:::
