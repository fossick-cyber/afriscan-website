::::section{id="cut-off-date" eyebrow="The cut-off date" title="What existed, and on what date"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
A resettlement plan published in January 2025 for a World Bank-financed road project in the DRC defines the cut-off date as follows: "C'est la date qui marque le début de l'opération de recensement des personnes et de leurs biens. […] les biens (maisons, champs, arbres…) mis en place après la date limite ne sont pas indemnisés" ([resettlement plan, 2025](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012225111023710/pdf/P1618771d5d78d0311a4241565cbe8c3429.pdf)). It is the date that marks the start of the census of people and their assets, and assets such as houses, fields and trees put in place after it are not compensated.

On that project the cut-off date was set three times, at 13 November 2021, 6 March 2023 and then 15 August 2024, and the emprise went from 9 m to 15 m, or 12 m in constrained areas. For another World Bank-financed project, a plan set the cut-off date at 2 July 2021, the first day of a census held from 2 to 22 July 2021, under the World Bank's Environmental and Social Standard 5 ([resettlement plan](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515007152278754/pdf/P17350600fff300a085280b5de04d67c91.pdf)).

When the emprise or the timetable changes, the cut-off date can be redefined: a dated image survey shows what existed at each date.

### Congolese law {#congolese-law}

- **Land tenure.** "Le sol est la propriété exclusive, inaliénable et imprescriptible de l'Etat": the land is the exclusive, inalienable and imprescriptible property of the State ([Loi n° 73-021 of 20 July 1973](https://www.leganet.be/Legislation/JO/2004/JO.01.12.2004.pdf), art. 53). According to the resettlement policy framework the World Bank published in 2013, the inquiry this law provides for includes a census of the people present on the land or carrying out an activity there, a description of the site and an inventory.
- **Expropriation.** According to the same document, Loi n° 77-001 of 22 February 1977 counts among expropriable rights "les droits de jouissance des communautés locales sur les terres domaniales", local communities' rights of use over State land.
- **Mining.** "En cas de déplacement des populations, l'opérateur minier est tenu préalablement de procéder à l'indemnisation, à la compensation et à la réinstallation des populations concernées": where people are displaced, the mining operator must first compensate and resettle them ([Mining Code, art. 281, since 2018](https://www.leganet.be/Legislation/Droit%20economique/Code%20Minier/Loi.18.001.09.03.2018.html)).
- **Electricity.** A servitude is preceded by a notification and a record of condition; a permanent emprise gives a right to compensation, including for "les occupants des terres rurales qui ont effectivement mis le fonds en valeur", occupants of rural land who have actually developed it ([Loi n° 14/011, arts 111 to 113](https://www.leganet.be/Legislation/Droit%20economique/Energie/Loi.14.011.17.06.2014.htm)).
- **ESIA.** The impact study includes "l'inventaire précis et détaillé de l'état initial du site, de son environnement naturel, socio-économique et humain", a precise and detailed inventory of the site's initial state and of its natural, socio-economic and human environment, and anyone may consult the reports ([Décret n° 14/019, arts 19 and 38](https://www.leganet.be/Legislation/Droit%20administratif/Environnement/D.19.019.02.08.214.htm)).

### Lenders' standards {#standards}

Where a project is financed by IFC, by a bank that applies the Equator Principles or by the World Bank, [IFC Performance Standard 5](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2010/2012-ifc-performance-standard-5-en.pdf) or the World Bank's Environmental and Social Standard 5 applies to resettlement. Both call for a census and an asset inventory closed at a cut-off date.

Our register supports the cut-off-date record: it establishes what the imagery showed at a known date. It replaces neither the census, nor the asset inventory, nor the consultation of affected people.

:::callout{tone="legal" title="Who decides"}
Who is eligible, and for what, is decided in the RAP, with the affected people and the competent authorities. Our surveys support the census and the asset inventory; they do not replace them, and they record no one.
:::
