  - q: We work on a mining lease. Who would we contract with?
    a: With a qualifying Ghanaian firm, which holds the contract under the Minerals Commission's local procurement list; AfriScan supplies the imagery analysis and reviewed registers to it. ### For a route or lease in Ghana

- **The regions and districts** the line, route or lease crosses, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The asset:** gas or product pipeline, transmission line and its voltage, railway, road or fibre route, or mining lease or concession.
- **The widths or rings to report:** the 15 m or 20 m protected strip for a 161 kV or 330 kV line, the corridor in your acquisition instrument or wayleave, the reserve width, or the ring around a lease.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs a Ghana-registered operator with a GCAA ROC, and near high-tension wires a special authorisation, so the proposal shows how the approvals fit the schedule. See [drone rules in Ghana](/gh/drone-regulations).
- **The date the record must reflect**, such as a cut-off date, a lease renewal or the date a line was built.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
- **For mine leases and upstream petroleum:** the Ghanaian firm or permit holder your contract needs, if you already have one.
