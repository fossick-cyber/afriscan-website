### For a route or site in Zimbabwe

- **The province and district**, and the rural district or urban council areas the route crosses, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The asset:** a power line and its voltage, with tower positions or wayleave polygons if you have them; a pipeline and its reserve width or polygon; or the boundary of a mining location, claims or lease.
- **The distances to report:** the S.I. 177 clearance for each voltage, the pipeline reserve and the 125 m band, the Mines and Minerals Act setbacks, a road or railway reserve width, or your own standard. Up to six distances in one survey.
- **Your own imagery:** whether your team already flies drones over the asset, and the dates of the latest orthophotos.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs CAAZ approvals, an Air Services Permit, security clearances and written approvals from landowners and local authorities, so the proposal shows their lead time. See [drone law in Zimbabwe](/zw/drone-regulations).
- **The date the record must reflect**, such as a cut-off date, a handover or the start of works.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
