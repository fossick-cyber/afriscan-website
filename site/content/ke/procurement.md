::::section{id="e-gp" tone="alt" eyebrow="Electronic procurement" title="The e-GP system"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
Public procurement in Kenya is moving onto the [e-GP system](https://egpkenya.go.ke/). The [Public Procurement Regulatory Authority](https://ppra.go.ke/) describes bidding, evaluation and awards moving "entirely online".

The Treasury Cabinet Secretary told the Senate that mandatory use took effect on 1 July 2025, with 1,543 procuring entities onboarded, 9,772 tenders published and 2,604 contracts processed ([KBC, 17 June 2026](https://www.kbc.co.ke/treasury-cs-defends-e-gp-rollout-citing-improved-transparency-efficiency-in-procurement/)). In September 2025 the High Court issued conservatory orders requiring manual and electronic bids to be accepted equally until a hearing ([KBC, 8 September 2025](https://www.kbc.co.ke/court-suspends-mandatory-e-procurement-system/)). Read the submission instructions in each tender.

State corporations and utilities also run their own supplier registration and prequalification alongside e-GP, and lender-financed programmes follow their lender's procurement rules, with notices on the [World Bank's projects site](https://projects.worldbank.org/) for the programmes it finances.

::::section{id="preferences" eyebrow="Citizen and local preferences" title="What section 157 of the PPADA requires"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
The [Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act 2015](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2015/33/eng@2022-12-31) (Cap. 412C), as consolidated after the 2022 amendments, sets four rules in section 157 that shape who can win a mapping or drone contract:

- **Exclusive preference for citizens** where the funding is wholly from the national government, a county government or a Kenyan body and the amount is below the prescribed threshold (s.157(8)(a)).
- **A margin of preference** for local goods and by local shareholding (s.157(8)(b)).
- **The 40 per cent rule:** "all foreign tenderers participating in international tenders" must "source at least forty percent of their supplies from citizen contractors prior to submitting a tender" (s.157(9)), as a mandatory preliminary evaluation criterion.
- **Reserved value:** at least 30 per cent of procurement value each year goes to youth, women and persons with disability (s.157(10)).

A **Local Content Bill 2025** is before Parliament. The President has directed that it be expanded to set a framework for small-scale trade, identifying activities that may be reserved for citizens, while Kenya "remains open to lawful foreign investment, employment and enterprise" ([KBC, 8 September 2026](https://www.kbc.co.ke/foreign-traders-given-90-days-to-regularise-immigration-status-businesses/)). Check its status before each tender.
::::
::::col
:::callout{tone="legal" title="In a proposal"}
For a tender that scores these rules, the proposal names the company that will contract, identifies the parts of the work sourced from citizen contractors, and answers each preference criterion in writing, so the evaluation committee can check it against the tender document.

::::section{id="drones" tone="alt" eyebrow="Drone work" title="Who may fly, and what to ask for"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
Commercial drone work in Kenya needs an RPAS Operator Certificate from the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority, which it issues only to a company registered in Kenya with its principal place of business there and a security clearance from the Ministry responsible for defence (Civil Aviation (Unmanned Aircraft Systems) Regulations 2025, regs 24 and 26). Everyone who engages in the operations needs a security clearance too (reg 4), every operation needs KCAA authorisation (reg 23), and aerial photography for mapping needs a month's written notice to the Director of Surveys (Survey Act s.42).

For evaluation, split any scope into its satellite or desk-based part, which needs no aviation approval, and its drone part, which needs all of them. Ask for the flying company's certificate number, expiry date and operations specifications, its security clearances, its insurance and the notice letter to the Director of Surveys.

Drone surveys are subject to the approvals and security clearances each job requires. Every AfriScan proposal with a drone element names the Kenyan company that will fly it.

::::section{id="data" eyebrow="Data and imagery" title="Processor terms and imagery licences"}
:::cards{cols="2"}
:::card{title="Data Protection Act terms" icon="lock" key="ke-data-protection"}
The Act reaches processors outside Kenya and lists "property details" among sensitive data. The contract should carry the regulation 24 processor terms, the authorisation of sub-processors under regulation 25, and where data is processed and stored.
:::
:::card{title="Imagery licences and credits" icon="satellite"}
Each proposal names the imagery the work will use, its capture dates and its licence terms. Open data is credited as its licence requires, and map-service basemaps are never delivered as survey imagery. See [data sources](/data-sources).
