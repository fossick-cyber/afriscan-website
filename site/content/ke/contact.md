### For a wayleave or site in Kenya

- **The route or site file:** KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, Shapefile, GPX or GeoPackage, or the wayleave or reserve polygons from your GIS.
- **The county or counties** it crosses, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The width to report:** from the gazette order, easement, wayleave agreement or resettlement action plan, for example 30 m or 40 m for a transmission line, or the road or railway reserve.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs a Kenyan operator certificate, security clearances, KCAA authorisation of the operation and a month's notice to the Director of Surveys, so the proposal shows the lead time. See [drone law in Kenya](/ke/drone-regulations).
- **The deadline**, and any notice-of-intention, Gazette or cut-off date the record must reflect.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
- **The report language:** reports are in English.
