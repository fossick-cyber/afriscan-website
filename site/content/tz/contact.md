### For a wayleave, reserve or site in Tanzania

None of this is needed to get in touch: it is what we'll ask about when we get back to you.

- **Region and district**, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The widths to report:** the wayleave in your own acquisition or agreement, the road reserve for the road class, the 30 m railway reserve, a safety zone, or your company standard. Up to six widths in one survey.
- **Any valuation or cut-off date**, and any notice already issued, so the imagery plan can match those dates.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work in Tanzania needs an operator with a Tanzanian ROC, a security clearance and TCAA's authorisation for each operation, so the proposal shows the lead time. See [drone law in Tanzania](/tz/drone-regulations).
- **What the register is for:** maintenance, a wayleave or reserve dispute, a new route, a resettlement baseline or lenders' monitoring.
- **Oil, gas or mining work:** tell us, because the proposal then sets out its local-content position under the Petroleum Act or the Mining (Local Content) Regulations.
