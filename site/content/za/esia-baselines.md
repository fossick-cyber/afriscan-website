::::section{id="esia-rap-baselines" tone="alt" eyebrow="Baselines for EAPs and consultancies" title="Layers for your own maps and reports"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
Environmental authorisation in South Africa runs under the National Environmental Management Act and the EIA Regulations 2014 (GN R.982, GG 38282), as amended in 2017, 2018, 2020 and 2021, and registration with EAPASA has been required of environmental assessment practitioners since 8 August 2022. The practitioner owns the assessment; what we supply is the land data it needs, early and dated.

For a project footprint, corridor or study area, we deliver a structure layer with reviewer categories, estimated households with the assumptions stated, settlement growth trends around the site, a land-use and land-cover baseline, a comparison of alternative sites or alignments, and slope, drainage and flood screening. Where your team has flown its own drone survey, we run the analysis on those orthophotos. Everything arrives as GeoPackage, GeoJSON, KMZ or Shapefile for your GIS team, with a PDF report where you want one.

Some projects arrive with less environmental process than others: compliant power lines inside the strategic transmission corridors are excluded from environmental authorisation under the [power-line standard (GN 2313)](https://www.dffe.gov.za/sites/default/files/legislations/nema_powerlinessubstationsdevelopmet_g47095gon2313.pdf), so the land baseline for them is needed from another source.

::::section{id="authorisation-compliance" eyebrow="Environmental authorisation" title="Roughly when, and roughly where"}
:::::columns{split="1-1"}
::::col
For audits, rectification and applications, we compare dated imagery of the site against the footprint in the authorisation:

- **Clearing and construction footprints** compared between dates, from satellite scenes and drone surveys.
- **Roughly when** clearing or construction happened, from the imagery history of the site.
- **Structures and activity inside the no-go buffers** or sensitive areas you define.
- **A pre-development land-cover baseline**, and the progress of rehabilitation afterwards.
::::
::::col
:::callout{tone="scope" title="What it shows, and what it doesn't"}
Imagery shows what was visible on the ground on each capture date. It supports an audit, an application or a response to a directive; it does not by itself prove compliance or non-compliance, and it does not replace the environmental control officer's inspection.
:::

::::section{id="cut-off-records" eyebrow="Lender-financed projects" title="Resettlement cut-off-date records"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
IFC Performance Standard 5 applies to projects financed by DFIs and Equator Principles banks, including transmission lines and independent power projects. South Africa has no single national resettlement regulation, so a project's resettlement plan combines national law with the lender's standard.

We produce a dated record of the structures in the project footprint around the cut-off date, with imagery, coordinates, IDs and reviewer categories, and re-survey the footprint afterwards so that structures built after the cut-off date are flagged and confirmed. The record supports your census and asset inventory and the IFC Performance Standard 5 cut-off-date records; it does not replace the census, set the cut-off date or determine eligibility.
::::
::::col
:::callout{tone="note" title="In the standard's own terms"}
IFC's guidance ties the cut-off date to the completion of the census and asset inventory. A dated structure register, made from imagery captured close to that date, gives the census team and later grievance reviews a fixed reference.

::::section{id="due-diligence" tone="alt" eyebrow="Before investment or acquisition" title="Land due diligence"}
Before financial close or an acquisition, we assemble the imagery history of the site, route or portfolio, a land-cover baseline, and the current structures on its rights of way, in its concession or inside its hazard zones. The history says what was built, cleared or changed, and roughly when; how much archive exists varies by place. It is not a title, tenure or valuation check.
