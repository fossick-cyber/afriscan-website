### For a servitude or site in South Africa

- **Province and municipality**, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The widths to report:** those in your servitude agreements and diagrams, a building line or company standard, or the SANRAL building restriction area. Up to six widths in one survey.
- **Your own imagery:** whether your teams already fly drones along the route, and the dates of the latest orthophotos.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs a UASOC operator, an Air Service Licence and landowner permission for each flight, so the proposal shows the lead time. See [drone law in South Africa](/za/drone-regulations).
- **What the register is for:** maintenance and planning, a wayleave, a route baseline, or a record that may be used as evidence, which raises POPIA questions to settle first ([POPIA and aerial imagery](/za/popia)).
