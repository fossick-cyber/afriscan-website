::::section{id="local-content" tone="alt" eyebrow="Oil and gas" title="Local content under the 2026 laws" lead="Mozambique published a new Petroleum Law and a Local Content Law on 3 June 2026. Both took effect on publication."}
:::::columns{split="1-1"}
::::col
### Lei n.º 8/2026 (Lei do Petróleo) {#petroleum-law}

- Foreign individuals or companies that provide services to petroleum operations **must associate with Mozambican individuals or companies** (art. 60(2)), with real economic substance (art. 60(3)) and local preference (art. 60(4)).
- The Instituto Nacional de Petróleo is the Autoridade Reguladora do Petróleo (art. 37).
- Resettlement costs fall on the rights holder, after prior consultation (art. 61).

[Lei n.º 8/2026, BR I Série n.º 104, 2.º Suplemento](https://www.inp.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/BR_104_I_SERIE_2.o_SUPLEMENTO_2026.pdf)
::::
::::col
### Lei n.º 9/2026 (Lei do Conteúdo Local) {#local-content-law}

- Applies to petroleum and gas projects and to the entities that contract with them, which must **pass the obligations on to their subcontractors** (art. 3).
- A Local Content Authority keeps the register of suppliers (*cadastro de fornecedores*).
- Partnerships need at least **20 per cent** of the share capital held by nationals (art. 17(5)), and national bids get a margin of preference.
- The **exclusivity regime** (art. 28) reserves the goods and services in Annex II for firms with at least 80 per cent national production factors, at least 20 per cent Mozambican capital and at least 50 per cent of the wage bill paid to Mozambicans. Annex II lists **serviços de topografia**, alongside land transport, onshore security, catering, banking, legal, insurance and accounting services.
- Sanctions include fines, contract cancellation and exclusion from tenders. The implementing regulations were due within 90 days; check whether they have been issued.

[Lei n.º 9/2026, BR I Série n.º 104, 3.º Suplemento](https://www.inp.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/BR_104_I_SERIE_3.o_SUPLEMENTO_2026.pdf)

:::callout{tone="scope" title="Where this service sits"}
AfriScan registers are remote-sensing analyses of structures, cleared ground and change, built from satellite imagery, open building datasets and imagery you supply. They are not topographic, cadastral or statutory surveys. Drone surveying for oil and gas work is closer to *topografia* and is likely to be treated as such. Each proposal separates the two, so your local-content team can apply the rules to each part.

::::section{id="drones" eyebrow="Drone work" title="Who may fly, and what each survey needs"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
IACM issues approvals for corporate and commercial drone operations **only to companies registered and domiciled in Mozambique** (DOS-09-2018 §8.3.3(a)). Each commercial operation needs a written IACM approval before it flies, registered aircraft with Certificates of Safety and qualified pilots.

Under [Lei n.º 6/2024](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/mz/2024/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2024-06-04-no-108.pdf), the survey itself needs the Minister of National Defence's authorisation (art. 13(1)(b)), and releasing the data to the client needs the authorisation of the systematic-cartography entity (art. 13(2)). The law applies to the client as an *entidade utilizadora* (art. 2(1)). A recent decree on licensing aerial work reserves it for national operators, as reported by [JLA Advogados](https://www.jlaadvogados.com/post/novo-regulamento-de-licenciamento-das-actividades-de-transporte-a%C3%A9reo-e-trabalho-a%C3%A9reo-em-mo%C3%A7ambique); whether it reaches drone work is not settled in the sources we checked.

Drone surveys are subject to the approvals and security clearances each job requires. Afridrone is working towards the operator approvals Mozambique requires, and every AfriScan proposal with a drone element names the company that will fly it and the authorisations each flight needs.

::::section{id="data" tone="alt" eyebrow="Data" title="Survey data, privacy and imagery licences"}
:::cards{cols="3"}
:::card{title="Drone data belong to the State" icon="lock"}
The original data of aerial coverage by private and foreign companies are State property, and after processing the operator deposits the coverage scheme, navigation data, camera calibration certificate, data copies and a processing report (Lei n.º 6/2024, art. 14). Put the deposit and the release authorisation in the contract.
:::
:::card{title="Privacy" icon="shield"}
Lei n.º 6/2024 protects privacy, image and honour (art. 6). Mozambique has no general data-protection law yet: the Council of Ministers approved a bill on 3 March 2026 and sent it to the Assembleia da República ([INTIC](https://intic.gov.mz/proposta-de-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-segue-para-debate-na-assembleia-da-republica/)); check its progress. Our registers record structures, not people, and carry no names.
:::
:::card{title="Imagery licences and credits" icon="satellite"}
Each proposal names the imagery the work will use and its capture dates. Open data is credited as its licence requires: Google Open Buildings (CC BY 4.0), Microsoft Building Footprints (CDLA-Permissive-2.0), © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) and Copernicus Sentinel. Map-service basemaps are never delivered as survey imagery.
:::

::::section{id="buyers" eyebrow="Public and private buyers" title="Supplier platforms and public procurement"}
:::::columns{split="1-1"}
::::col
State bodies buy under the public-procurement regulation, and UFSA keeps the single supplier register, the *Cadastro Único* (Decreto n.º 79/2022, art. 43), which can be consulted free of charge ([UFSA](https://www.ufsa.gov.mz/cadastro_unico.php)).

Large LNG and energy projects run their own supplier platforms, as do the state companies [ENH](https://pfornecedores.enh.co.mz/) and [EDM](https://fornecedores.edm.co.mz). If your process runs through one of them, name it in your request.

### What we send for your supplier file

:::checklist
- A written proposal: scope, method, imagery plan, deliverables and schedule
- An imagery statement: sources, capture dates, licence terms and credits
