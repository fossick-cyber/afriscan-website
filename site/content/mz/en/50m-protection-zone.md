## What the Land Law says {#land-law}

The Land Law, [Lei n.º 19/97, de 1 de Outubro](https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/486/2635/file/Lei%20de%20Terras.pdf), divides the public domain into total and partial protection zones. Article 8 lists the partial protection zones (*zonas de protecção parcial*, ZPP). For linear infrastructure, the key item is article 8(g): the land occupied by motorways and four-lane roads, and by *instalações e condutores aéreos, superficiais, subterrâneos e submarinos de electricidade, de telecomunicações, petróleo, gás e água, com uma faixa confinante de 50 metros de cada lado*: overhead, surface, underground and submarine installations and conduits for electricity, telecommunications, oil, gas and water, with an adjoining strip of 50 m on each side.

The same article sets other strips that matter to land-holding companies:

| Article 8 | Partial protection zone |
|---|---|
| (c) | 100 m of land along the coast |
| (d) | 100 m around springs |
| (e) | 250 m around dams and reservoirs |
| (f) | Railways, with 50 m on each side of the axis |
| (g) | Oil, gas, water, electricity and telecommunications installations and conduits, with 50 m on each side; roads, with 30 m for primary and 15 m for secondary and tertiary roads |
| (h) | 2 km along the land border |
| (i) | Airports and aerodromes, with a 100 m strip |
| (j) | Military and other defence and security installations, with a 100 m strip |

## What the zone means {#what-it-means}

Protection zones are public domain (art. 6). **No land-use right (DUAT) can be acquired in them**; the law allows only special licences, *licenças especiais*, for specific activities (art. 9). A DUAT cannot therefore be the basis for building inside the 50 m strip, and anything that stands there sits on public-domain land.

That does not make each structure in the strip a simple case. Some predate the infrastructure, and some have special licences. The strip tells an operator where the law protects the line; it does not settle, by itself, what happens to what already stands there.

## How the sector laws build on it {#sector-laws}

**Petroleum.** The Petroleum Law of June 2026 repeats the strip for petroleum infrastructure: *as áreas que circundam as infra-estruturas numa faixa de 50 metros, consideram-se zonas de protecção parcial* ([Lei n.º 8/2026, art. 75(3)](https://www.inp.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/BR_104_I_SERIE_2.o_SUPLEMENTO_2026.pdf)). It leaves the safety zone to regulation (75(4)), provides compensation for damage to crops, buildings and improvements (75(5)) and allows rights of passage (75(7)). The rights holder bears resettlement costs after prior consultation (art. 61).

**The Pande–Temane corridor.** [Decreto n.º 36/2001, de 20 de Novembro](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz50003.pdf) sets a 50 m partial protection zone (art. 1) and a **200 m safety zone** (art. 2) on each side of the Pande–Temane gas pipeline corridor and around its processing and compression unit, and requires the project operator's prior consent for infrastructure there (art. 3). It was made under the petroleum law of the time; whether it continues unchanged under the regulation the 2026 law calls for is a point to confirm with counsel.

**Electricity.** The Electricity Law sets an administrative servitude of *até 50 metros confinantes … contados a partir do eixo da linha*: up to 50 m, counted from the line's axis, recorded on the concession ([Lei n.º 12/2022, art. 43(4)](https://arene.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Lei-de-Electricidade-2022.pdf)). The width depends on voltage and setting (43(5)), a safety zone lies inside it (43(6)), and the servitude is registered in the Cadastro de Terras and the Conservatória do Registo Predial (43(7)). Resettlement and compensation rules apply (43(8)), but **no compensation is owed where the holders or owners acquired their rights after the line was built** (43(10)).

:::callout{tone="legal" title="Two ways of counting"}
The Land Law describes a strip on each side of the installation; the Electricity Law counts from the line's axis. For a buried pipeline and an overhead line, those can be different lines on the ground. Which one a given width is measured from is a question for your counsel and your concession terms.
:::

## What the draft reform would change {#reform}

The Council of Ministers approved a Land Law proposal for submission to the Assembleia da República on 28 October 2025 ([AIM](https://aimnews.org/2025/10/29/governo-aprova-proposta-de-lei-de-terras/)). The published June 2024 draft ([ante-projecto, art. 15(3)](https://www.agricultura.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CRPNT-do-Ante-Projecto3-da-Lei-de-Terra-vJunho.2024-CIRCULAR-v1.pdf)):

- keeps the 50 m strip for conduits;
- widens the coastal strip to 200 m and the strip around springs to 150 m, and sets roads at 30 m and 15 m on each side;
- keeps 100 m around airports and military installations, and adds **250 m around military servitudes**;
- would let the distances, including the conduit strip, be reduced or increased (art. 15(4)).

Until the Assembleia adopts a text, Lei n.º 19/97 is the law in force. Check the adopted text before relying on any new width.

## What it means for an operator {#operators}

**Know what stands in the strip.** A register of the structures inside the 50 m zone and in a band outside it, each measured to the line, tells the servitude team where the strip is clear, where it is filling and which stretches to visit first.

**Record the date.** The Electricity Law turns compensation on when rights were acquired, and the Petroleum Law provides compensation for damage in the zone. A register tied to dated imagery, compared with earlier imagery where it exists, is the record those questions come back to.

**Plan regularisation and resettlement on facts.** Where structures must move, resettlement follows Decreto n.º 31/2012 and, on lender-funded projects, IFC Performance Standard 5. A dated register sizes the census and shows what arrived after the cut-off date: see [resettlement cut-off-date baselines](key:resettlement-cut-off-baselines).

**Route new lines around the land.** For a new pipeline or line, counting the structures along each option within the same strips weighs the land affected before the route is fixed.
