### For a line, reserve or concession in Botswana

- **The districts** the route or area crosses, and the nearest villages or towns, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The asset:** a power line and its voltage, a road or rail reserve, a bulk-water pipeline, a buried cable route, or a mining licence or permit area.
- **The widths or zones to report:** the servitude width of each line, the road reserve, or your licence boundary with any ring around it. Up to six widths in one survey.
- **The land it crosses**, if you know it: tribal land, state land or freehold farms.
- **Your own imagery:** whether your teams already fly drones, and the dates of the latest orthophotos.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs CAAB-registered drones, an operator with an RPAS Operator Certificate, an authorisation for each operation and security vetting, so the proposal shows the lead time. See [drone law in Botswana](/bw/drone-regulations).
- **What the record is for:** maintenance, a development near a line, a new route, a licence application, a cut-off date or inspections.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
