### For a servitude, route or licence area in Namibia

- **The regions** the line, road or licence area covers, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The asset:** a power line and its voltage, a road or rail reserve, a pipeline, or a mining, petroleum or energy licence area, as a line or a polygon.
- **The widths or distances to report:** those in your wayleave agreements, the 60 m road reserve or the 100 m building restriction along trunk and main roads, the 100 m and 300 m consent distances around planned workings, or your own or your lender's standard. Up to six in one survey.
- **Restricted areas:** any diamond restricted area, national park or other area where access needs a permit, so the proposal plans those parts from satellite imagery.
- **Your own imagery:** whether your teams already fly drones along the route or over the site, and the dates of the latest orthophotos.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Drone work needs an NCAA Letter of Approval for each operation and the landowner's permission, so the proposal shows the lead time. See [drone law in Namibia](/na/drone-regulations).
- **What the register is for:** maintenance and bush clearing, a wayleave, a route choice, a baseline before construction or a cut-off date, and who should receive the results.
