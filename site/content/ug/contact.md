### For a route or site in Uganda

None of this is needed to get in touch: it is what we'll ask about when we get back to you.

- **The route or site file:** KML, KMZ, Shapefile, GeoJSON, GPX or GeoPackage, or the right of way or site boundary as a polygon.
- **The districts** the line or site crosses, so we can check the imagery that exists for the area.
- **The widths to report:** a pipeline right of way and the 6 m and 30 m bands; a 10 m right of way plus 25 m of wayleave either side; a road reserve and building line; or your own.
- **Satellite only, or drone detail too.** Each drone operation in Uganda needs UCAA approval, Chief of Defence Forces clearance, a line-ministry letter and a district sign-off, so the proposal shows what the flights rely on. See [drone law in Uganda](/ug/drone-regulations).
- **The image dates or cut-off date** the record must reflect, and your deadline.
- **Who should receive the results**, so they go only to the contacts you name.
- **For petroleum and public contracts:** any national content, NSD or PPDA reservation requirement, so the proposal can address each one.
