::::section{id="summary" eyebrow="In short" title="What this website collects" width="prose"}
:::checklist
- The details you type into the contact form, when you choose to send it
- Two small preferences stored in your own browser, for the country-site suggestion
:::

:::checklist{tone="no"}
- No analytics or visitor-tracking tools
- No advertising cookies: the site's pages and scripts set no cookies at all
- No fonts, scripts or maps loaded from outside services while you browse
- No mailing list: we do not add you to one when you write to us
:::
::::

::::section{id="contact-form" tone="alt" eyebrow="The contact form" title="What happens when you send a request" width="prose"}
The form asks for your name, work email, organisation, role and country, what needs surveying, and optionally its size, your timing, what you need, a message and how you heard about us. It also sends, without showing them, which section of the site you wrote from and, if you followed a link from an industry or solution page, which one, so the reply can be relevant.

When you press send, your browser sends these details to **FormSubmit** (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service, which forwards them by email to the AfriScan mailbox. Our mailbox is a Google Gmail account. FormSubmit's own [privacy terms](https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf) describe how it handles form data.

We use what you send only to reply to you, to scope the work you asked about and to prepare a proposal. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone outside AfriScan and Afridrone except where needed to answer your request or where the law requires it.

::::

::::section{id="browser" eyebrow="In your browser" title="The country-site suggestion" width="prose"}
The site can suggest a country section (Mozambique, South Africa or Nigeria) that matches where you are browsing from. To do that:

- the page asks our hosting provider which country your connection comes from, and receives only a two-letter country code, which the site does not keep;
- if that answer is not available, the page uses your device's time-zone setting instead, without sending it anywhere;
- the answer is kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab, and your choice of site (or "stay on this site") is kept in your browser's local storage so the suggestion is not shown again.

Both items stay on your device. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The suggestion never redirects you.
::::

::::section{id="hosting" tone="alt" eyebrow="Hosting" title="How the site is served" width="prose"}
The website is served by **Cloudflare Pages**. As with any website, the hosting provider receives the technical details your browser sends with each request, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. The site uses these details for nothing else. Fonts, scripts and images are served from the site itself.
::::

::::section{id="project-data" eyebrow="Project data" title="Routes, imagery and results" width="prose"}
Route files, boundaries, imagery and results you share for a project are handled as the proposal and agreement for that work set out. We never publish a client's route, imagery or results without written permission. The only route shown on this site, a high-pressure gas pipeline in Mozambique, is shown with the route owner's permission.

Our surveys map land and assets: structures, cleared ground, excavations and tracks. We do not identify people, and we do not follow people or vehicles.
::::

::::section{id="your-rights" tone="alt" eyebrow="Your choices" title="Asking about your details" width="prose"}
You can ask us which details from your enquiry we still have, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them, by sending a request through the [contact form](/contact) and saying it is about privacy. Where a data-protection law gives you further rights, such as South Africa's POPIA or Nigeria's Data Protection Act, we will answer your request in line with it.

We will update this notice when the website changes what it collects, for example if analytics are ever added, and the date below will change with it.
