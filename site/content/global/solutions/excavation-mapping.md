::::section{id="what" eyebrow="What it is" title="The digging that comes before the problem"}
:::::columns{split="2-1"}
::::col
On a pipeline or cable right of way, third-party works are one of the main threats to the line: a trench for a water pipe, a borrow pit for a road, foundations for a new building, earthworks for a farm dam. They are visible from above as fresh digging, spoil heaps and new tracks, often before anyone reports them.

On a mining right, surface excavation by others changes the ground you are answerable for: pits, disturbed ground, spoil and water-filled excavations inside the concession or around its edge, often growing between visits.

We map those features between dated surveys, flag each new or growing site with its location, size on the imagery and distance to your line or boundary, and a reviewer confirms each one before it reaches you. The result is a list of places for your field teams to check. It tells them where to look; it does not replace them.
::::
::::col
:::callout{tone="scope" title="Land, never people"}
We describe excavations, pits, spoil, water-filled pits and tracks. We never count, describe or identify the people involved, and we never attribute ground disturbance to anyone. Whether works are authorised is for you and the authorities to decide.
:::

:::steps{style="list"}
:::step{title="Imagery to match the features"}
Very-high-resolution satellite scenes or drone orthophotos for individual excavations; open satellite data and global disturbance alerts for larger disturbed areas across a wide concession. Radar comparisons show larger earthworks even under rainy-season cloud.
:::
:::step{title="Detection tuned to your ground"}
Detection of excavations, spoil and pits is tuned to your imagery and checked on part of your area before it runs on the rest.
:::
:::step{title="Flag and confirm"}
New and growing features are flagged between dated surveys and confirmed by a reviewer, with before-and-after views. New tracks and access roads are mapped alongside.
:::
:::step{title="Tell your field teams"}
A notice after each survey lists the sites to check, by chainage or zone, with coordinates your teams can navigate to.
:::
:::

:::::columns{split="1-1"}
::::col
### What it shows
:::checklist
- Excavations, spoil, pits and tracks visible at the surface on dated imagery
- Where they appeared or grew between surveys
- Their distance to your line or boundary
:::
::::
::::col
### What it does not show
:::checklist{tone="no"}
- Leaks, taps, theft, corrosion or the condition of any pipe or cable
- Underground workings, or shafts under cover
- Features under tree canopy, which can be missed
- Anything about the people involved
:::
