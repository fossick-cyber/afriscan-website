::::section{id="overview" eyebrow="As of 26 September 2026" title="What applies in each country" lead="A short orientation to the land rules our registers are measured against and the drone rules that shape a survey plan. It summarises public rules for information and is not legal advice; the sources are linked."}
:::cards{cols="3"}
:::card{title="Mozambique" icon="map" eyebrow="Português · English"}
**Land.** The Land Law makes the land within 50 m on each side of oil, gas, water, electricity and telecommunications lines a partial protection zone, where no land-use right (DUAT) can be acquired, only special licences ([Lei n.º 19/97, arts. 8 and 9](https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/486/2635/file/Lei%20de%20Terras.pdf)). The Electricity Law sets a servitude of up to 50 m from a line's axis ([Lei n.º 12/2022, art. 43](https://arene.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Lei-de-Electricidade-2022.pdf)).

**Drones.** Flights are regulated by the civil aviation authority ([IACM](https://www.iacm.gov.mz/aeronaves-nao-tripulados/)). Aerial surveys also need authorisation under [Lei n.º 6/2024, de 4 de Junho](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/mz/2024/mz-government-gazette-series-i-dated-2024-06-04-no-108.pdf), including for handing the survey data to the client (art. 13).
:::
:::card{title="South Africa" icon="map" eyebrow="English"}
**Land.** Pipelines, power lines and bulk-water lines run in servitudes, and servitude diagrams are the work of registered professional land surveyors. Our registers support that work; they are not cadastral surveys.

**Drones.** Commercial drone work needs an operator certificate (UASOC) from the South African Civil Aviation Authority and an Air Service Licence ([Civil Aviation Regulations Part 101, GN R.3170, 2023](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202303/48228rg11556gon3170.pdf)).
:::
:::card{title="Nigeria" icon="map" eyebrow="English"}
**Land.** The Oil Pipelines Act lets buildings and cultivation be restricted within a set distance, not exceeding 100 feet, of a pipeline's licensed land ([s.12](https://lawsofnigeria.placng.org/view2.php?sn=425)). Power-line rights of way are set by voltage in the NESIS Regulations 2015, from 11 m to 50 m, with no structures under the line ([NESIS](https://nemsa.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/NESIS-Regulations-2015-1-1.pdf)).

**Drones.** Commercial drone work needs an RPAS Operator Certificate from the NCAA, with security clearance built into the process ([Nig.CARs 2023 Part 21](https://ncaa.gov.ng/media/wabn2axn/nigcars-part-21-remotely-piloted-aircraft-system.pdf)).
:::
:::

:::callout{tone="legal" title="Drone permits and AfriScan"}
We never assume a permit. Afridrone is working towards the operator approvals each country requires, and every drone proposal sets out the permits and authorisations that particular flight needs and how they fit the schedule. Satellite-based work involves no drone flight.
:::
